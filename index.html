At GPS, girls are at the center of everything we do. Our curriculum is intentionally tailored specifically for the ways girls learn best—empowering them to question boldly, solve problems, and lead.

METHODOLOGY

Girl Centered

In an all-girls environment, girls occupy every leadership position, pilot every lab experiment, ask every question, and command every stage without hesitation.

Hers to hold

For more than 118 years, GPS traditions have woven an enduring thread connecting generations of girls. From May Day pageantry to Senior Ring presentations, Cat-Rat pairings, and Chapel gatherings, our traditions celebrate the joy of growing up together.

May Day Pageantry

A cherished century-old GPS tradition celebrating senior leadership and dance.

Senior Ring Ceremony

Passing down the gold onyx GPS ring, symbol of lifelong sisterhood.

Cat-Rat Mentorship

Seniors ("Cats") welcoming new sixth graders ("Rats") into the sisterhood.

Bruiser Spirit Week

Unbridled energy, blue-and-black class cheers, and campus unity.

Opening Convocation

Starting every school year united on our historic front lawn.
